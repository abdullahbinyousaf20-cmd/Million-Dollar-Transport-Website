Open-Deck Freight

Flatbed Equipment

Flatbed is the workhorse of the open-deck world and it's what we know best. Steel coils, structural beams, lumber, construction equipment, machinery, prefab panels — if it's heavy, oversized, or needs to be loaded from the top or side, we have the equipment and the drivers to handle it safely.

Our flatbed drivers are experienced with load securement regulations (49 CFR Part 393), tarping procedures for weather-sensitive freight, and the attention to detail that open-deck freight requires. We don't treat flatbed like a commodity — it's a specialty, and we run it like one.

Enclosed General Freight

Dry Van Equipment

When your freight needs to be fully enclosed and protected from the elements, dry van is the answer. General freight, packaged consumer goods, industrial supplies, retail products, and anything that ships on pallets or skids runs efficiently in a 53' dry van.

We support both live load/unload and drop-and-hook operations. Our dry van drivers are experienced with distribution center procedures, appointment freight, and time-sensitive delivery windows.
