Equipment

Flatbed & Dry Van — The Right Trailer for Every Load

Flatbed

Open-Deck Freight Specialists

Flatbed is our core specialty. Steel, machinery, lumber, oversized industrial equipment — if it loads from the top or side and needs to be secured with chains and straps, we know how to handle it. Our drivers are experienced with tarping, load securement regulations, and special permit loads.

Capacity: Standard 48', extendable 53', up to 48,000 lbs payload. Tarping available for weather-sensitive freight.

Full Flatbed Specs
Dry Van

Enclosed General Freight

For palletized goods, packaged consumer products, retail freight, and any load that needs to ship enclosed and protected from the elements — dry van is the answer. We run 53' trailers and support drop-and-hook operations.

Capacity: 53' trailers, up to 44,000 lbs. Air-ride suspension available. Drop-and-hook and live unload supported.
