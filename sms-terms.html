SMS & Messaging Terms

This page describes how Million Dollar Transport uses text messaging to communicate with customers, drivers, and business contacts.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 Last Updated: January 1, 2024

These SMS Messaging Terms ("SMS Terms") govern the text message communications between Million Dollar Transport LLC ("Million Dollar Transport," "we," "us," or "our") and individuals who have provided their phone number and consented to receive SMS messages in connection with our services. These terms supplement our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Quick Summary: We text you only about your freight or dispatch — no marketing spam, ever. Reply STOP to stop. Reply HELP for assistance. Msg & data rates may apply.

1. Program Description

Million Dollar Transport operates an SMS communications program for the purpose of delivering transactional and service-related messages to customers, shippers, freight brokers, and owner-operators who have opted in to receive text communications. All messages are directly related to the services you are actively inquiring about or receiving from us.

We do not operate a promotional SMS marketing program. No unsolicited marketing messages will be sent.

2. Types of Messages You May Receive

If you have opted in to SMS communications from Million Dollar Transport, the types of text messages you may receive include:

  • Load updates: Notifications regarding pickup times, delivery windows, delays, or other changes to an active load;
  • Dispatch confirmations: Confirmation of load bookings, rate confirmations sent via text, and related dispatch communications for owner-operators;
  • Documentation coordination: Requests or confirmations related to bill of lading, rate confirmations, proof of delivery, or other freight documentation;
  • Service status updates: Updates regarding the status of a freight quote inquiry, dispatch onboarding, or active service engagement;
  • Check-call coordination: Status check requests or updates related to an active load or delivery;
  • General service communications: Responses to questions or inquiries you have sent us via phone, form, or text, where a text reply is the most efficient means of communication.

All messages are transactional or service-related in nature. We will not send promotional offers, advertising campaigns, or unsolicited marketing content via text message.

3. How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Million Dollar Transport through any of the following methods:

  • Web Form: By submitting the contact or quote request form on our website and providing your phone number, you acknowledge that you may be contacted via text message in connection with your inquiry. Consent language is displayed on the form at the point of submission.
  • Verbal or Written Consent: When signing up for our dispatch service or establishing a carrier or broker relationship with us, you may provide verbal or written consent to receive SMS communications related to that service engagement. You will be clearly informed of this consent at that time.
  • Direct Request: You may request SMS communications from us directly during a phone call or email exchange, at which time we will confirm your consent.

Consent to receive SMS messages is never a condition of purchasing or receiving any service from Million Dollar Transport.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on the nature and volume of your active engagement with our services. There is no fixed or guaranteed message frequency.

  • Dispatch clients (owner-operators): May receive multiple messages per day when active loads are being coordinated, including load offers, pickup confirmations, check-call requests, and delivery updates.
  • Shippers and brokers: Will receive messages only in direct connection with active freight inquiries or loads, which may range from occasional to multiple messages per load.
  • General inquirers: May receive one or a small number of messages in connection with responding to your submitted inquiry.

5. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. The charges assessed by your mobile carrier for sending and receiving text messages depend on your specific mobile plan. Million Dollar Transport is not responsible for any fees or charges imposed by your mobile carrier. If you are unsure whether your plan includes unlimited texting, please contact your mobile carrier before opting in.

6. How to Opt Out (STOP)

You may opt out of SMS communications from Million Dollar Transport at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us.

After texting STOP:

  • You will receive a single confirmation text acknowledging that you have been unsubscribed;
  • No further SMS messages will be sent to your number from our program;
  • Opting out of SMS does not affect your ability to receive service from us via phone or email;
  • If you wish to re-subscribe to SMS communications in the future, please contact us directly to restore your opt-in status.

If you need assistance related to our SMS communications, reply HELP to any text message you receive from us. You will receive a response with basic information and our contact details. You may also contact us directly:

8. No Third-Party Sharing of SMS Consent

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Your mobile phone number and opt-in consent will never be sold, rented, or transferred to any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

9. Supported Carriers

Our SMS program is supported by major U.S. wireless carriers. Carrier support may vary, and not all features may be available on all carriers or with all devices. We are not responsible for service interruptions or delays caused by your carrier.
